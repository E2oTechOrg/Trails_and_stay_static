About Us

Built by People Who Know Kodaikanal Best

We started out running cab transfers and valley-view tours for visitors arriving at Kodaikanal Bus Station, and grew into a full trip-planning service after guests kept asking us to sort out their stay and sightseeing too.

Every itinerary we put together is built around the same idea: fewer logistics for you, more time actually seeing Kodaikanal — its lakes, viewpoints, waterfalls, and village trails.
