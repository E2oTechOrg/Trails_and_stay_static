Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters

How We Handle Your Information

Your details are safe with us.
Last updated: September 2026

Trails & Stays ("we", "us", "our") offers jeep adventure rides, cab service, and accommodation in Kodaikanal. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or book with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to plan and confirm your trip:

  • Contact details: name, phone number, and email address
  • Booking details: travel dates, pickup and drop location, group size, and the service or stay you choose
  • Identity details required by law for accommodation check-in (for example, a valid photo ID)
  • Messages you send us through the contact form, phone, or WhatsApp
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device, and pages visited
2

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm, manage, and deliver your bookings for jeep rides, cabs, and stays
  • To contact you about pickup timing, itinerary changes, or cancellations
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support
  • To meet legal and accommodation record-keeping requirements
  • To improve our website and services
3

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to serve you:

  • With our drivers and stay hosts, limited to what they need to complete your trip
  • With service providers who help us run the website or communicate with you
  • With authorities when required by law or to protect safety and rights
4

Cookies & Third-Party Services

Our website may use cookies and similar tools to make pages load properly and understand how visitors use the site. It may also load third-party content such as Google Fonts and maps, which have their own privacy policies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

6

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7

Your Rights

You may ask us to:

  • Show you the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it
  • Stop using your information for enquiry follow-ups or updates
8

Children's Privacy

Our website is meant for adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without a parent or guardian's involvement in the booking.

9

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the updated date shown at the top.
