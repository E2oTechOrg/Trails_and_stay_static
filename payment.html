How Payments Work

Clear pricing. No surprises.
Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how payments work for jeep adventure rides, cab service, stays, and tour packages booked with Trails & Stays in Kodaikanal. Please read it along with our Terms & Conditions and Cancellation Policy.

1

Accepted Payment Methods

We accept the following:

  • UPI
  • Bank Transfer
  • Cash

Payment details are shared directly by our team when you confirm your booking. Please pay only to the account or UPI ID we give you.

2

Advance Payment

  • An advance may be required to confirm your booking, especially for stays, multi-day packages, and pre-booking services such as night camping and bonfire
  • The advance amount will be told to you at the time of booking
  • Your booking is confirmed only after we receive the advance and acknowledge it
3

Balance Payment

The remaining amount is payable at the time agreed with you, usually before or on arrival, pickup, or check-in. Services may be withheld if the balance is not paid as agreed.

4

Pricing & What's Included

  • All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR)
  • Rates are as quoted at the time of booking and may be higher in peak season and on holidays
  • Tour packages include driver bata, fuel, parking, and toll unless stated otherwise
  • Entry fees for tourist places, meals, and personal expenses are not included
5

Extra Charges

Any changes you request after booking, such as extra hours, added stops, extra guests, or a longer stay, may cost extra. We will tell you the additional amount before it is added to your bill.

6

Payment Confirmation & Receipts

After you pay, please share a screenshot or transaction reference with us so we can match it to your booking. You can ask for a payment receipt or bill at any time.

7

Failed or Delayed Payments

If money is deducted from your account but we haven't received it, contact us with your transaction details. Such amounts are usually returned by your bank within a few working days. We are not responsible for delays or failures on the side of your bank or payment app.

8

Refunds

Refund eligibility, amounts, and timelines are covered in our Cancellation Policy. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method or the account you provide.

9

Payment Safety

  • We will never ask for your card PIN, UPI PIN, or OTP
  • Confirm payment details with our team by phone if you are unsure
  • We do not store your card or banking credentials on this website
10

Payment Disputes

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, please contact us as soon as possible with your booking and payment details. We will review the issue and reply promptly.
