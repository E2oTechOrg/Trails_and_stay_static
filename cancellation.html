Cancellations & Refunds

Plans change. We'll work it out with you.
Last updated: September 2026
The earlier you tell us, the more we can refund. Please contact us by phone to cancel or change a booking.

This policy applies to jeep adventure rides, cab service, stays, and tour packages booked with Trails & Stays in Kodaikanal. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions and Payment Policy.

1

How to Cancel

  • Call us on +91 96773 04131 with your name, booking date, and service booked
  • Your cancellation counts from the time we receive and acknowledge your request
  • Please ask for a confirmation of the cancellation for your records
2

Refund Schedule

The refund on your advance or paid amount depends on how far ahead of the service date you cancel:

Time before service / check-in Refund
7 days or more Full refund (minus payment charges, if any)
3 to 6 days 50% refund
Less than 3 days No refund
No-show No refund
3

Peak Season & Special Bookings

During peak season, long weekends, and holidays, and for pre-booking services such as night camping and bonfire experiences, stricter terms may apply. Any special cancellation terms will be told to you at the time of booking.

4

Changes & Rescheduling

  • You can request a change of date, subject to availability
  • Please ask as early as possible; last-minute changes may not be possible
  • Any difference in rates for the new date is payable by you
5

Cancellation by Us

If we have to cancel or change a service because of heavy rain, landslides, road closures, government restrictions, safety concerns, or unavailability, we will inform you as soon as possible. You can choose between a new date, an alternative service, or a refund of the amount paid for the affected service.

6

Weather & Unused Services

  • Bad weather alone does not entitle you to a refund if the service can still be safely provided, though we will adjust the route or timing where possible
  • Services already started or partly used, and unused meals, stops, or nights, are not refundable
  • Early check-out from a stay is not refundable
7

How Refunds Are Paid

  • Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method or the account you give us
  • Refunds are usually processed within 7 to 10 working days after approval
  • Your bank or payment app may take extra time to show the amount
8
